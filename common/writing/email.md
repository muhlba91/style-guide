# Email

Abstract features only, from about 95 sent mails of mid-2024 to late 2026. Every rule is `lean`: tiebreaker for tone and structure, never the sole basis for a rule in another family. Not used: salutation and closing formulas (recipient-driven), pre-thanks lines, smileys, exclamation marks, comma before "and", contractions, length targets for docs, anything before 2024. Examples are synthetic.

## MAIL-01 · lean · common · observed

- Rule: Open with the point after the salutation; add a pleasantry only in a reply.
- Source: about 80 of 95 mails.
- Check: judgment

## MAIL-02 · lean · common · observed

- Rule: State identifiers, dates and time windows explicitly, and offer availability as a list.
- Good: `Order 1234, delivery on 12 March; I am available Tuesday or Thursday afternoon.`
- Source: identifiers and dates stated explicitly in about 45 of 70 non-trivial mails; availability offered as a list in about 15.
- Check: judgment

## MAIL-03 · lean · common · observed

- Rule: Keep paragraphs to 1 to 3 sentences, ask one thing per paragraph, and number the questions when there are two or more.
- Source: about 90 of 95 mails for paragraph length; 4 of 5 multi-question mails numbered.
- Check: judgment

## MAIL-04 · lean · common · observed

- Rule: Report a defect or complaint neutrally: situation, expected versus actual, concrete request; no blame or emotion words.
- Source: 6 of 6 complaint mails.
- Check: judgment

## MAIL-05 · lean · common · observed

- Rule: Use no bold or italics in plain-text mail; em dashes follow PRIN-11.
- Source: 0 of about 95 bodies; a spaced hyphen or `->` appears rarely. Partial reads only.
- Check: judgment

## MAIL-06 · lean · common · observed

- Rule: Apologize only for a real own error, once and briefly.
- Source: 2 of 95 mails.
- Check: judgment
